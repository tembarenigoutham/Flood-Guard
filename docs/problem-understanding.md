# Problem Understanding

Floods occur frequently in states like Bihar and Assam due to heavy rainfall and river overflow.

## Current Issues
- Manual monitoring of water levels
- Delayed flood warnings
- Poor communication between authorities and citizens
- Lack of real-time data

## Impact
- Loss of life
- Damage to property and crops
- Difficulty in evacuation and rescue operations

## Need
A system is required to monitor flood conditions in real-time, predict risks early, and provide timely alerts to reduce damage.
